# Community conduct

Keep discussions useful and welcoming. Critique the code or proposal, not the person. Give newcomers room to ask questions. Disagreement is fine; harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and sexualized conduct are not.

Do not post other people's private information, credentials or private conversations. Keep support threads focused on the project, and avoid repeated promotion or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Contact the maintainer through a private GitHub report for concerns involving private information; otherwise flag the content through GitHub's reporting tools.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests and project discussions.
